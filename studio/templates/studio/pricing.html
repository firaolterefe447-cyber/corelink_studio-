Scope

  • Work outside the agreed scope, quoted before we start
Payment, tax & timing

How paying us works

Payment schedule

Projects under ETB 300,000: 50% to start, 50% on delivery. Larger projects: 40% start, 30% at the midpoint demo, 30% on delivery. Monthly services are paid in advance.

Tax and invoices

Withholding tax

Companies in Ethiopia may withhold tax on service payments above the legal threshold, currently around 3%, and give us a withholding receipt. Rates changed in 2025, so confirm with your accountant. Tell us your TIN so we invoice correctly.

How to pay

Bank transfer or Telebirr. Send us the receipt and we confirm within one working day.

Timeline

The clock starts when we receive the deposit and your content. Late content or late approvals move the delivery date by the same number of days.

If you stop

You pay for the work completed so far, and you receive everything finished to that point.

FAQ

Questions we get often

What is included in a package?

Design, development, testing, launch, and the support period listed on the package. Hosting, domain and add-ons are separate so you only pay for what you use.

How do I pay?

50% to start and 50% when we deliver. Larger projects can be split into milestones. We accept bank transfer and Telebirr.

Why is the price a range?

Because projects differ. Number of pages, languages, integrations and design depth change the work. After our call you get one fixed price, and it does not change unless you change the scope.

Can I change a package?

Yes. Packages are starting points. Tell us what to add or remove and we will adjust the quote.

Who owns the website or app?

You do. When the final payment is made, you receive the source code and access to all accounts.
